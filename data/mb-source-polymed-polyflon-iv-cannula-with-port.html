Description:
Introducing the Polymed Polyflon IV Cannula with Port and Wings, a reliable solution designed to optimize intravenous (IV) therapy and fluid administration in medical settings.

Key Features:

  1. High-Quality Construction: The Polymed Polyflon IV Cannula is constructed from high-quality materials, including a polyurethane catheter and wings, ensuring durability, biocompatibility, and optimal flow rates for reliable venous access.

  2. Integrated Injection Port: Equipped with an integrated injection port, this IV cannula allows for convenient medication administration, blood sampling, or infusion of compatible fluids without the need for additional needle sticks.

  3. Secure Fixation Wings: The IV cannula features secure fixation wings that provide stability and support during insertion and securement to the patient's skin.

  4. Transparent Hub: Constructed with a transparent hub, the IV cannula allows for visual inspection of blood flashback upon venipuncture, enhancing procedural visibility and facilitating successful cannulation on the first attempt.

  5. Flexible Tubing: The IV cannula is equipped with flexible tubing that allows for ease of movement and positioning during IV procedures. The pliable tubing minimizes the risk of kinks or obstructions, ensuring uninterrupted fluid flow and optimal patient comfort during infusion therapy.

Benefits:

  • Convenient Medication Administration: The integrated injection port allows for convenient medication administration, blood sampling, or infusion of compatible fluids without the need for additional needle sticks, enhancing patient comfort and treatment management efficiency.

  • Secure and Comfortable: With secure fixation wings and a smooth, tapered catheter tip, the Polymed Polyflon IV Cannula ensures secure placement and patient comfort during IV procedures, minimizing the risk of complications and enhancing procedural reliability.

  • Enhanced Procedural Visibility: The transparent hub of the IV cannula allows for visual inspection of blood flashback, promoting procedural visibility and facilitating successful venipuncture on the first attempt, reducing patient discomfort and procedural time.
