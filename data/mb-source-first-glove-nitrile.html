Description

First Glove Nitrile Examination Gloves in powder-free blue are designed to deliver outstanding protection, comfort, and flexibility for various medical and industrial applications. These gloves ensure superior strength and resistance, making them a dependable choice for professionals requiring precision and durability. Suitable for medical examinations, laboratory work, and other high-hygiene environments.

Key Features

  • Excellent barrier protection for medical and laboratory use
  • Ambidextrous design with a beaded cuff for easy donning
  • Polymer-coated with textured fingertips for enhanced grip
  • Minimum tensile strength of 14 MPa for superior durability
  • Available in multiple sizes from XS to XXL
  • Acceptable Quality Level (AQL): 1.5 (exceeds ASTM D6319 standard)

Yes, First Glove Nitrile Examination Gloves are powder-free. Powder-free gloves eliminate the risk of powder particles contaminating sterile fields, medical devices, or patient wounds, making them suitable for sensitive procedures in medical examinations, laboratory work, and high-hygiene environments where contamination control is critical.

These gloves are made from nitrile, a synthetic rubber polymer that is inherently latex-free. Nitrile provides excellent barrier protection and is suitable for professionals with latex sensitivities or those working in latex-free clinical environments.

AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) 1.5 indicates that these gloves exceed the ASTM D6319 standard for defect rates, ensuring superior quality control during manufacturing. A lower AQL rating means fewer defects are acceptable, providing assurance of consistent barrier protection and durability for clinical applications.

These gloves have a minimum tensile strength of 14 MPa, which ensures superior durability and resistance to tearing during medical examinations and laboratory procedures. Higher tensile strength reduces the risk of glove failure during use, providing reliable protection for both the wearer and the patient.

These are non-sterile, single-use examination gloves designed for medical examinations, laboratory work, and other high-hygiene environments. Non-sterile gloves are appropriate for general clinical settings where a sterile field is not required, such as routine patient examinations and diagnostic procedures.

The gloves feature an ambidextrous design with a beaded cuff for easy donning and removal, and polymer-coated textured fingertips that provide enhanced grip. These design elements improve handling precision and reduce the risk of accidental glove slippage during medical and laboratory work.
