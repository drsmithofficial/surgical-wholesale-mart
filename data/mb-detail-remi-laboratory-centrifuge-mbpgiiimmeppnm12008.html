The Remi R 8C Plus Laboratory Centrifuge is a high-performance benchtop centrifuge engineered for routine laboratory applications. It is suitable for use in hospitals, clinical diagnostics, research facilities, and educational institutions. This centrifuge facilitates the efficient separation of biological samples such as blood, serum, plasma, urine, and other fluids, making it ideal for hematology, biochemistry, and microbiology workflows.

Operating at a maximum speed of 6000 RPM and a maximum RCF of 5070 "g", the Remi R 8C Plus ensures effective sample separation and quick processing. It features a digital LED display for precise monitoring of speed and time settings. The unit incorporates a brushless induction motor with a variable frequency drive for stable speed output and smooth operation.

The Remi R 8C Plus is designed to accommodate a wide variety of rotors and reduction adaptors, offering versatility for diverse centrifugation tasks. For enhanced operator safety, the centrifuge includes automatic rotor identification, built-in imbalance detection with auto-shutdown, and a safety lid interlock to prevent opening during operation.

The Remi R 8C Plus achieves a maximum RCF of 5070 g at its top speed of 6000 RPM. This RCF rating is critical for determining the centrifugal force applied to samples during separation, ensuring effective pelleting of biological materials such as blood cells, serum proteins, and microbial cultures in routine diagnostic workflows.

The centrifuge includes automatic rotor identification, a safety lid interlock that prevents the lid from opening during operation, imbalance detection that stops centrifugation and displays an error if samples are unevenly distributed, and motor overload protection. These features work together to ensure operator safety and prevent sample spillage or equipment damage.

The R 8C Plus features a brushless induction motor with variable frequency drive and an inverter fault detection system with auto shutdown. This design maintains stable speed output even when laboratory power supply fluctuates, ensuring consistent and reliable sample separation across different clinical environments.

The centrifuge supports a wide variety of rotors and reduction adaptors to accommodate different sample volumes and tube types. With a maximum capacity of 400 ml and automatic rotor identification, the system allows selection of appropriate rotors based on your specific sample volume requirements for hematology, biochemistry, and microbiology applications.

The centrifuge offers a 7-segment LED display for speed monitoring, a digital countdown timer with 0-99 minute range, smooth and soft start to minimize sample temperature rise, and dynamic brake for quick deceleration. It also recalls the last set parameters, making it efficient for repetitive analyses in high-throughput diagnostic labs.
