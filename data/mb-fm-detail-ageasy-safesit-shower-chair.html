The AGEasy Shower Chair is a sturdy, comfortable bathroom chair for seniors designed to make bathing safer, easier, and more independent for older adults. This shower chair for elders features a wide, stable seating surface, anti-slip legs, and easy height adjustments, reducing the risk of slips and falls in wet areas.

For individuals with mobility limitations, balance concerns, or post-surgical needs, this bath chair for elders provides a secure and reliable solution. Its thoughtfully contoured seat, integrated drainage system, and reinforced back support ensure consistent comfort and stability throughout bathing.

It also functions as an excellent bathing seat for elders who require additional safety and reassurance during their hygiene routine, helping them maintain dignity, confidence, and independence.

Key Features

  • Heavy-duty, strong yet lightweight aluminium frame with anti-rust coating
  • 5-level height-adjustable legs (73–83 cm)
  • Anti-skid rubber feet that grip wet tiles and keep the chair stable
  • Wide, ergonomically designed seat with built-in drainage holes
  • Removable backrest and padded armrests for support while sitting down or standing up
  • Weight capacity: up to 100 kg

Specifications

  • Dimensions: 63 x 21 x 9 cm

Step 1: Adjust the height – place the chair on a flat surface, press the spring button on each leg and adjust all four legs to the same height. Ensure the buttons pop out fully through the holes and the chair is level and stable.
Step 2: Position the chair – place it in the bathroom and ensure all anti-skid rubber feet are firmly touching the floor; the chair should not wobble or tilt.
Step 3: Sit down safely – hold both armrests firmly, slowly lower yourself onto the seat and sit with your back against the backrest.
Step 4: Bathe comfortably – use the armrests to maintain balance; drainage holes in the seat let water flow through.
Step 5: Stand up carefully – hold both armrests, push up slowly using your arms and legs, and ensure stable footing before stepping away.
Step 6: After use – rinse the chair with clean water, wipe dry with a soft cloth and allow it to air-dry.

Use cases: Senior citizens requiring safe bathing support; post-surgery recovery (knee, hip or leg surgery); arthritis, joint pain or muscle weakness; limited mobility or balance issues; assisted bathing with caregiver support; independent bathing with added safety.
