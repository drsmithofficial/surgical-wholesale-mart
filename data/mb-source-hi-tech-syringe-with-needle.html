Composition:

Hi-Tech Syringe consists of medical-grade plastic for the barrel and a stainless steel needle. These components are manufactured using advanced techniques to ensure they meet high standards for safety and optimal performance.


Direction to Use:

  1. Preparation: Ensure the syringe is sterile and intact before use.

  2. Filling: Draw the required medication into the syringe by pulling back the plunger.

  3. Administration: Position the syringe at the desired injection site and insert the needle.

  4. Injecting: Push the plunger down smoothly to administer the medication.

  5. Retracting: After use, activate the retractable feature to safely dispose of the needle, minimizing injury risks.

The Hi-Tech Syringe features a retractable needle mechanism that significantly reduces needlestick injury risk. After medication administration, the needle retracts safely into the syringe barrel, minimizing exposure to bloodborne pathogens and enhancing worker protection during disposal.

The needle uses a push-and-twist luer lock connection that secures firmly onto the syringe tip. To assemble, push and twist the needle clockwise from the hub side until it seats tightly. This design prevents needle loosening during transportation and ensures a reliable seal during medication administration.

The Hi-Tech Syringe is crafted from high-quality materials engineered for biocompatibility and durability in clinical use. The construction ensures reliability and long-lasting performance across repeated medication administration procedures in healthcare settings.
