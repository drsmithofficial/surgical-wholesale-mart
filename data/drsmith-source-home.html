ABOUT DR. SMITH

A Legacy of
Care & Innovation

Registered in 2024, Dr. Smith brings together over 65 years of family experience in healthcare products. Our journey began with surgical instruments and expanded across generations into hospital furniture and medical equipment. Today, we focus on Smart Premium Products—combining premium quality, practical functionality and economical pricing.

  • Comprehensive range across ICU, OT, diagnostics, neonatal care and hospital furniture
  • Serving government and private hospitals, nursing homes and medical colleges
  • Focused on quality, installation support, after-sales service and dependable supply

WHAT WE DO

Smart Premium
Products for Healthcare

Our Smart Premium portfolio spans MGPS & modular OT, hospital furniture, medical equipment, surgical instruments, medical consumables and hospital linen. We combine premium quality, practical functionality and economical pricing, with our own manufacturing range continuing to grow.

  • ICU beds, OT tables, lights, monitors, ventilators, anesthesia and general hospital furniture
  • Modular OT and medical gas pipeline products, with partner-supported project coordination
  • Diagnostics, laboratory, neonatal care, CSSD and many other healthcare essentials
