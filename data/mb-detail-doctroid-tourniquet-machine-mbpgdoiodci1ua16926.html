The Doctroid Tourniquit Machine - Model DIT-105 is a compact, fully automatic tourniquet system engineered for clinical precision and reliability in surgical and medical settings. This device is designed to temporarily restrict blood flow to a specific limb, supporting various medical procedures.

Operating directly on AC power, the DIT-105 facilitates real-time adjustable cuff pressure during procedures, ensuring consistent performance. It is supplied with a set of 2 autoclavable silicon cuffs, offering versatility and ease of sterilization for repeated use.

Equipped with a user-friendly backlit LCD display, the DIT-105 allows for easy monitoring of elapsed time and pressure. Safety is enhanced through its built-in auto-deflate functionality and automatic maintenance of set pressure values, contributing to safe and effective performance. The unit's portable and lightweight design further supports its application in diverse clinical environments.

A tourniquet is a device used to apply pressure to a limb, temporarily restricting blood flow.

Properly applied tourniquets should not cause significant pain, but patients may experience slight discomfort.
