Types of Temperature Guns: 

Temperature guns, also known as infrared thermometers, come in various types, each designed:

1. Handheld Infrared Thermometers: Portable and widely used for various applications. Some feature laser sighting to outline the measurement area.

2. Pocket/Stick-Type Infrared Thermometers: Extremely compact, designed for easy carrying in a pocket.

3. Infrared Thermocouples: Small, cost-effective sensors that are self-powered and mimic thermocouple sensors.

4. Fixed Mount Infrared Thermometers/Transmitters: Stationary devices suitable for industrial processes. Mounted in a fixed position for continuous monitoring.

5. Two Colour-Ratio Thermometers: Measure the ratio of infrared energy at two wavelengths for accurate temperature data. Based on the principle of relating visible colour to temperature.

Accuracy and Calibration of Forehead Thermometer:

If an infrared thermometer provides inaccurate readings, recalibration is necessary, especially when compared to a calibrated thermometer measuring the same object in identical conditions. It's essential to consider external factors like emissivity affecting the accuracy of IR readings.

 

When calibrating an infrared thermometer:

  • Account for External Factors: Be mindful of external influences on measurement accuracy.
  • Ambient Room Temperature: Calibrate at ambient room temperature, preferably around 22°C.
  • Acclimatize the Device: Allow the device time to acclimatize to the environment before calibration.
  • Quick Measurements: Take multiple measurements swiftly to prevent temperature fluctuations.

How to use Non-Contact Thermometers? 

  • Determine the required field of view based on target size and distance. Choose a thermometer with a suitable distance-to-spot ratio.

  • Specify the relevant temperature range and assess mounting needs, especially for fixed applications.
  • Consider response time, environmental conditions, mounting limitations, and any viewing port or window requirements.
  • Evaluate the thermometer's signal processing capabilities to meet specific needs.

FAQ’s:

1. What is the warranty period for IR thermometers? 

The warranty period for IR thermometers varies by manufacturer and model. Typically, warranties range from 1 to 3 years. It's important to check the specific warranty terms provided by the manufacturer at the time of purchase.

 

2. How accurate are Infrared thermometers?

Infrared thermometers are generally accurate within a range of ±0.5 to ±2 degrees Celsius (±1 to ±4 degrees Fahrenheit). However, accuracy can be influenced by factors such as emissivity settings, distance-to-spot ratio, and environmental conditions. Calibration and adherence to proper usage guidelines contribute to optimal accuracy.

 

3. Does infrared thermometer work on water?

Infrared thermometers can measure the surface temperature of water, but they may not accurately measure the internal temperature. The reflective nature of water can affect readings, and the device primarily captures the surface temperature. For precise measurements, contact methods or specialized thermometers are recommended.

 

4. How often should infrared thermometers be calibrated?

Infrared thermometers should be calibrated regularly, with the frequency depending on factors such as usage, criticality of measurements, and manufacturer recommendations. As a general guideline, calibration every 6 to 12 months is common to ensure accurate and reliable temperature readings.

 

5. Are there industry standards for infrared thermometers?
