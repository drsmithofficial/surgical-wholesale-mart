The Buffalo Filter PlumePen™ Surgical Smoke Evacuation ESU Pencil (Non-Stick Blade) is a specialized electrosurgical unit (ESU) pencil designed to effectively evacuate surgical smoke during electrosurgical procedures. Equipped with a non-stick blade, it minimizes the risk of tissue adhesion, ensuring smooth and efficient operation. The pencil integrates seamlessly with smoke evacuation systems, providing a comprehensive solution for maintaining a clear surgical field and protecting the surgical team from inhaling harmful smoke byproducts.

Key Features:

  • Non-Stick Blade: Reduces tissue adherence, enhancing operational efficiency and safety.
  • Integrated Smoke Evacuation: Directly connects to smoke evacuation systems, ensuring immediate removal of smoke at the source.
  • Ergonomic Design: Offers a comfortable grip, allowing for precise control during procedures.
  • Disposable: Designed for single-use to maintain hygiene and prevent cross-contamination.
  • Compatibility: Compatible with standard electrosurgical generators and smoke evacuation systems.
