The AccuSure Stethoscope (ST01) is an acoustic diagnostic instrument engineered for the accurate detection and auditory assessment of internal body sounds. It is designed for effective auscultation of heart, lungs, and other internal sounds, facilitating the clear transmission of physiological sounds for detailed clinical assessment.

This stethoscope features an aluminum chrome-plated chest piece, which is crucial for optimal sound transmission and clarity. Weighing 180g, the instrument's lightweight construction contributes to user comfort during prolonged examination periods. The durable build, including seamless PVC tubing and an epoxy powder-coated chest ring, ensures resistance to wear and tear for long-lasting performance.

The AccuSure Stethoscope (ST01) is suitable for general physical assessment and diagnosis in various clinical environments. Its compact and portable nature makes it practical for use in hospitals, clinics, and during ward rounds.
