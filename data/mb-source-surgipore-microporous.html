What's in the Box

1 x 12 Surgical Tape

Key Features:

  • Microporous Structure for Breathability:
    Surgipore Microporous Surgical Tape features a unique microporous design that allows air circulation, promoting breathability. This key feature minimizes the risk of skin maceration and ensures a comfortable experience for patients during extended wear.

  • Secure and Gentle Adhesion:
    The surgical tape is formulated to provide secure adhesion without causing skin trauma. The gentle yet effective bonding ensures that the tape adheres firmly to the skin, securing dressings or medical devices in place without compromising patient comfort.

  • Easy to Tear for Convenient Application:
    Surgipore Microporous Surgical Tape is designed for ease of use, allowing for quick and convenient application. The tape is easy to tear by hand, eliminating the need for scissors and ensuring efficient and precise dressing changes.

  • Optimal Dimensions for Versatile Use:
    With a width of 1/2 inch and a length of 9.1 meters, each roll of Surgipore Microporous Surgical Tape provides optimal dimensions for versatile use. This makes it suitable for various medical applications, including securing dressings, tubing, and providing light compression.

  • Benefits:

  • Gentle on Skin, Strong on Adhesion:
    Surgipore Microporous Surgical Tape strikes a balance between being gentle on the skin and providing strong adhesion. This quality is especially crucial in ensuring patient comfort while maintaining the integrity of dressings or securing medical equipment.

  • Breathable and Comfortable Wear:
    The microporous structure of the tape enhances breathability, reducing the risk of moisture accumulation and skin irritation. This breathable feature ensures a comfortable and irritation-free experience for patients, even during prolonged wear.

  • Convenient and Efficient Application:
    The easy-to-tear design simplifies the application process, allowing healthcare professionals to apply the tape efficiently without the need for additional tools. This convenience is advantageous in various medical settings, saving time and ensuring precise application.
