The White Westinghouse 4 in 1 Dehumidifier - 20 Litre (AWHD20L) is engineered to manage environmental humidity and air quality in various professional settings. This multifunctional device operates as a dehumidifier, air purifier, and laundry dryer, incorporating an in-built water tank for comprehensive environmental control. It is designed to mitigate excess moisture, thereby preventing the proliferation of mold, mildew, and musty odors, which is critical for maintaining hygienic conditions and protecting sensitive equipment in healthcare and laboratory environments.


Capable of extracting up to 20 Litres of moisture per day under optimal conditions (30°C, 80% RH), the AWHD20L is suitable for areas up to 2000 Cubic Ft. Its robust design ensures efficient operation across a temperature range of 5°C to 32°C, utilizing eco-friendly R290 refrigerant. The unit features an integrated air-purifying filter to trap dust, allergens, and airborne particles, contributing to cleaner indoor air quality.


This dehumidifier is built to conform to international standards such as CE and ISO9002, affirming its suitability for demanding professional applications. It is particularly beneficial for maintaining low humidity levels in pharmaceutical storage, laboratories, and areas housing sensitive electronic or laser equipment, including those used for eye operations, to prevent moisture-related damage.


Key Features : 

Multifunctional Operation: Functions as a dehumidifier, air purifier, and laundry dryer with an in-built water tank.
High-Capacity Moisture Removal: Extracts up to 20 Litres of moisture per day (at 30°C, 80% RH) for effective humidity control.
Integrated Air Purification: Features an air-purifying filter to capture dust, allergens, and airborne particles, enhancing air quality.
Quiet Operation: Operates at a low noise level of 45 dB(A) for minimal disruption in clinical or professional environments.
Safety and Convenience Features: Includes an auto-shutoff for a full water tank, auto-frost control, and an auto-restart function after power interruptions.
Regulatory Compliance: Conforms to International Standards such as CE and ISO9002.
Portable Design: Equipped with smooth-gliding caster wheels for easy relocation within facilities.
