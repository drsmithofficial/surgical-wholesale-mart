1 x 3 Instrument

The Vaishanav Surgical Nasal speculum thudichum set of 3 (S,S) is a fundamental instrument designed for secure and comfortable nasal examinations and reliable diagnostics during nasal procedures. This Thudichum Nasal Speculum is routinely utilized in outpatient settings for comprehensive examinations of the nose. It is also employed in various nasal procedures including anterior rhinoscopy, foreign body removal, nasal packing, and septal surgeries.


Crafted from stainless steel, this set features a unique design with two flanges that allow for precise insertion into the nostril during anterior rhinoscopy, enhancing the efficiency of diagnostic procedures. The smooth blades of the speculum are designed to gently dilate the nostril, improving visualization of the nasal cavity while minimizing tissue trauma during clinical procedures.
