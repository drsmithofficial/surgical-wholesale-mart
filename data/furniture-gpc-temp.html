Hydraulic C-Arm Operation Table

Model No. GHF155.01
Request a Quote Request a Catalog

Technical Specifications:

  • Overall length of table top : 1930 mm
  • Overall width of table top : 530 mm
  • Height adjustment : 730-980 mm
  • Lateral tilt both sides : 20 degree
  • Trendelenburg / Rev. trend : 25 degree
  • Back section : 60 degree
  • Head and leg section interchangeable (Manually)
  • Head section 90 degree up and down
  • Floor locking manually

Standard Accessories :

  • Side support with pads – 1 pair
  • Shoulder support with pads -1 pair
  • Knee crutches with assembly – 1 pair
  • Water proof rubber mattress
  • Anaesthesia screen rod – 1 pc
  • Arm rest with pads – 1 pair

Optional - Orthopaedic Attachment

  • Hydraulic five-section surgical table designed especially for Orthopaedic, Neurology, Urology and ERCP Procedure
  • Radio-translucent table top with eccentric pillars
  • Full length C-Arm compatible, having all surgical positions i.e. raising/lowering, trendelenburg, reverse trendelenburg, lateral tilt, flex/re-flex & chair position
  • Interchangeable head and leg sections
  • All positions operated through cranks fitted under the head end of the table In-built kidney bridge
  • Supplied complete with standard accessories and mattress
  • Constructed of 304 stainless steel
  • Equipped with a large translucent top, for C-arm use
