What's in the Box

1 x Instrument

The Vaishanav Surgical Vaginal Speculum Cusco Small is a meticulously designed, bivalved, self-retaining instrument engineered for comprehensive gynecological examinations and procedures. This speculum is primarily utilized to gently separate the vaginal walls, providing healthcare professionals with clear and unobstructed visualization of the vaginal canal and cervix. Its design facilitates accurate diagnostics and supports various clinical interventions, including Pap smears, intrauterine device (IUD) insertions, cauterization of vaginal erosion, and colposcopic examinations.


Crafted from surgical grade stainless steel, the Cusco speculum ensures reliability and is designed for repeated use after proper sterilization. The instrument features a side screw mechanism, allowing for secure blade adjustment and stable positioning during examinations, which contributes to both practitioner efficiency and patient comfort. Its smooth edges and adjustable design prioritize patient well-being during vaginal examinations.


Key Features : 

Bivalved, self-retaining design for clear visualization during vaginal and cervical examinations, including Pap smears and IUD insertions.
Small size with blade dimensions of 1-1/4″ x 3-1/2″ (3.2cm x 8.9cm) for appropriate anatomical fit.
Constructed from surgical grade stainless steel, ensuring durability and reusability after sterilization.
Equipped with a side screw mechanism for secure blade adjustment and stable positioning, enhancing patient comfort and diagnostic accuracy.
Conforms to ISO 9001, CE-Quality Mark, ISO 13485, and FDA standards for medical devices.
