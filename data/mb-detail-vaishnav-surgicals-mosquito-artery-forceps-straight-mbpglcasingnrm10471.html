The Vaishnav Surgicals Mosquito Artery Forceps Straight 5inch is a critical hemostatic instrument designed for precise control of blood vessels and delicate tissues during various surgical procedures. These specialized forceps are utilized by surgeons to clamp small arteries and manipulate sutures, ensuring effective hemostasis and tissue management in the operating room.


Crafted from medical-grade stainless steel, this instrument features a "straight" design with a "5inch" length, providing optimal balance and reach for intricate applications. The jaws are equipped with serrated tips, ensuring a secure yet atraumatic grip on vessels and tissues, while the integrated ratchet locking mechanism allows for consistent pressure and enhanced surgical control.


The Mosquito Artery Forceps Straight 5inch is suitable for a range of surgical applications, including pediatric, plastic, and vascular surgeries, where fine manipulation and precision are paramount. Its robust construction allows for reusability and compatibility with repeated sterilization cycles, ensuring durability and adherence to stringent clinical hygiene standards.

These forceps are constructed from stainless steel, which provides durability, corrosion resistance, and compatibility with standard sterilization protocols used in surgical settings.
