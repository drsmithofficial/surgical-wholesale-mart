The Doctroid Vein Finder - Infant is a medical device designed specifically for locating veins in newborns to 5-year-old patients. It effectively illuminates superficial veins, facilitating easier venipuncture and reducing discomfort during medical procedures. This device is an essential tool for healthcare professionals in various settings, including the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU).



Utilizing advanced trans-illumination technology, the vein finder effectively illuminates superficial veins. It employs a combination of red and near-infrared light to enhance vein visibility across various skin tones. The compact, O-shaped design, combined with a powerful LED light, aids in precise vein visualization.



The device's portable and lightweight design allows for easy transport and use in diverse medical settings. Its gentle and non-invasive nature minimizes discomfort for infants, making it suitable for sensitive patients. The built-in rechargeable battery ensures the device is always ready for use, reducing downtime.

The Doctroid Vein Finder consists of a durable housing that encases the LED light and rechargeable battery. The device is designed to withstand frequent use in clinical environments, ensuring reliability and longevity.

  1. Preparation: Ensure the device is fully charged using the included Android charger.
  2. Positioning: Hold the vein finder over the area of interest, positioning the O-shaped design for optimal visibility.
  3. Activation: Turn on the device to activate the LED light.
  4. Visualization: Adjust the position until the veins become visible through the illuminated area.
  5. Venipuncture: Once the veins are identified, proceed with the venipuncture as per standard medical procedures.
  • For professional use only; ensure proper training before use.
  • Do not use broken or irritated skin.
  • Keep the device clean and disinfected to maintain hygiene standards.
  • Store in a cool, dry place when not in use.
