The Tynor Foldable Commode Chair, Universal L 35, is engineered to provide a portable and accessible toileting solution for individuals with diverse healthcare needs, particularly those with limited mobility due to injuries, post-operation recovery, or other conditions. This commode chair is designed to support patients who are unable to use a traditional bathroom, facilitating the maintenance of bowel discipline and promoting independence in daily living activities.

Constructed with a rigid steel frame, the commode chair ensures a sturdy and durable product. It features a chrome finish, a molded polymer seat, a seat cover, and armrests, contributing to both comfort and aesthetics. The robust materials used in its construction provide stability and reliability for users.

This commode chair is versatile for use in various environments, including home, travel, or healthcare facilities, offering a secure and reliable toileting experience. Its foldable design allows for easy storage and transport, making it a convenient option for individuals requiring a portable toileting aid, especially during travel or in confined spaces.

The Tynor Foldable Commode Chair has a load-bearing capacity of up to 110 kg, supported by a rigid steel frame that provides structural integrity for safe and stable use across diverse patient populations.

The foldable design enables compact storage and easy transport between clinical areas, making it practical for facilities with space constraints or those requiring mobility between multiple treatment zones. This portability does not compromise the chair's structural stability during use.

The commode chair features a rigid steel frame with a chrome finish and a molded polymer seat. The seat cover and armrests are designed to enhance comfort while maintaining durability and ease of cleaning in clinical environments.

The commode chair is equipped with a removable sanitary container, enabling straightforward waste management and facilitating hygiene protocols in clinical and institutional settings.

The universal size and portable design make this chair suitable for use in physiotherapy clinics, rehabilitation centers, home care settings, and healthcare facilities where patients require accessible toileting solutions with mobility support.
