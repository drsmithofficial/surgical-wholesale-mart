GD-S600 Hydraulic Dental Unit
GD-600 HYDRAULIC DENTAL UNIT:A Symbol of Premium Excellence.
Its core highlight lies in the hydraulic drive system, ensuring smoother and more stable operation to meet diverse clinical needs. Equipped with an automatic full waterline disinfection system and imported water and air pipes, it guarantees safety and reliability. The self-developed 10-hole tri-color oral light illuminates every detail. With exceptional hydraulic technology, it leads the way in delivering a new era of dental care experiences.
Parameters
Gladent-Italian cooperation, making imported dental unit no longer expensive.
Automatic pulse pipe disinfection can effectively prevent cross infection.
Disinfectant can be used to disinfect the whole pipeline and the internal circuit of strong or weak suction.
Butterfly metal backrest. As close as possible to the patient.
The thin and narrow backrest provides an idea posture for doctors and patients.
