What's in the Box

1 x Thermometer, 2 x AAA Batteries

The MCP Infrared Thermometer is a non-contact medical device designed for rapid and hygienic temperature measurement of adults and babies. This device allows for temperature readings without physical contact, thereby minimizing the risk of cross-infection in clinical and home settings. Beyond human body temperature, it is also capable of measuring the temperature of objects and ambient room environments.


Engineered for ease of use, the thermometer provides quick measurements within approximately one second. Its non-contact operation is effective at a measurement distance of 1 to 15 cm, with optimal accuracy achieved within 5 cm. The device features a clear digital display and offers temperature readings in both Celsius and Fahrenheit units.


Key Features : 

Non-contact infrared technology for hygienic temperature measurement of body, objects, and room environments.
Rapid temperature acquisition, providing results within approximately one second.
Measures body temperature within a range of 32.0°C to 42.9°C (89.6°F to 109.2°F) with an accuracy of ±0.2°C.
Features a three-color backlight display and an audible fever alarm to indicate temperature status.
Includes a memory function capable of storing up to 32 previous readings for tracking temperature trends.
Equipped with an auto shut-off function after approximately 7 seconds of inactivity to conserve battery life.
CE Certified for compliance with European medical device directives.

The MCP Infrared Thermometer has an accuracy of ±0.2°C for body temperature measurements. This precision ensures reliable temperature readings for clinical decision-making in hospital, clinic, and diagnostic settings.

The thermometer operates effectively at distances of 1 to 15 cm, with optimal accuracy achieved within 5 cm of the forehead. This non-contact measurement prevents cross-infection and eliminates the need for surface cleaning between patients.

The thermometer measures body temperature from 32.0°C to 43°C (90°F to 109.4°F) and ambient/object temperature from 0°C to 60°C (32°F to 140°F). It operates reliably in clinical environments with ambient temperatures between 10°C and 40°C.

The device delivers temperature results in approximately 1 second, enabling rapid patient screening and assessment in high-volume clinical settings. An auto-shutdown feature activates after 7 seconds of inactivity to conserve battery power.

The thermometer features a digital display with backlight color indicators that vary based on body temperature, helping clinicians quickly identify fever status. Temperature readings can be displayed in both Celsius and Fahrenheit units to suit institutional preferences.

The device operates on 2 × 1.5V AAA batteries, which are included with the product. The auto-shutdown feature after 7 seconds of inactivity helps extend battery life during routine clinical use.
