What's in the Box

1 x Instrument

The Vaishnav Surgicals Allice Tissue Forcep is a specialized surgical instrument designed for precise tissue manipulation across various medical disciplines. This forcep is engineered to securely hold, grasp, and manipulate dense or fragile tissues, including fascia, muscles, or organs, during surgical procedures. It serves as an indispensable tool in general surgery, gynecological procedures, abdominal surgeries, and orthopedic interventions.



Featuring a length of 5 inches, this Allice Tissue Forcep provides an optimal balance between maneuverability and stability for delicate tissue handling. Its design incorporates interlocking serrated jaws with sharp teeth, which ensure a firm, non-slip grip on tissues while minimizing excessive damage. A ratcheted locking mechanism on the handle allows surgeons to maintain a controlled, distributed tension across the tissue edge, enhancing surgical control and accuracy.



Constructed from quality materials, this instrument is built for longevity and consistent performance in rigorous surgical environments. The surgical-grade stainless steel construction ensures corrosion resistance and allows for 100% autoclavability at 134°C, facilitating effective sterilization and reuse. Vaishnav Surgical Co. operates as an ISO 9001:2008 certified company, reflecting a commitment to quality management in the manufacturing of medical instruments.
