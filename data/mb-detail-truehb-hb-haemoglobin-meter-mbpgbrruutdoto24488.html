What's in the Box

The TrueHb Hb Hemoglobin Meter is a portable diagnostic device engineered for accurate and rapid hemoglobin measurement at the point of care. This device is intended for in vitro diagnostic use. It delivers reliable results within seconds from a small blood sample.


Utilizing advanced reflectance photometry technology, the TrueHb meter enables healthcare professionals and users to quickly assess hemoglobin levels for identifying anemia and monitoring treatment progress. It functions as a Point of Care Testing (POCT) device, dedicated to the quantitative testing of total hemoglobin concentration in blood. The meter is lightweight and compact, weighing 60 grams, and is compatible with fresh capillary or venous blood specimens.


Key Features : 

Quantitative testing of total hemoglobin concentration in blood
Utilizes reflectance photometry technology
US Patented technology (2020)

The TrueHb Hemoglobin Meter delivers hemoglobin results within 15 seconds from the time the blood sample is applied to the test strip. This rapid turnaround makes it suitable for point-of-care testing in clinics, hospitals, and health camps where quick assessment is critical for clinical decision-making.

The device uses capillary blood samples obtained from a fingertip puncture using a lancet. It requires only a small blood sample for testing, making it minimally invasive and practical for repeated monitoring in both clinical and home health settings.
