What's in the Box

1 x 25 Infusion Set

Features:


  • Transflow® (SS-3063)
  • Specially designed vented infusion set.
  • Made from clear, transparent medical grade P.V.C and has a flexible drip chamber.
  • Comes with a sharp spike for better penetration of glass/plastic bottles.
  • Disc type fluid filter at the bottom of chamber filters any particulate matter in the I.V fluid.
  • Provided with super smooth kink resistant tube for unobstructed flow.
  • Provided with latex free "Y" type injection port made up of silicone for additional medication.
  • Efficient roller controller ensures better flow control.
  • Luer lock for secure connection to all standard devices.
  • Sterile, individually packed.
  • Micro-Drop Technology for Precision:
    The infusion set incorporates advanced micro-drop technology, enabling
    precise control over the flow rate of intravenous fluids. This is
    particularly crucial in medical scenarios where accurate fluid
    administration is paramount, allowing healthcare providers to tailor
    treatments to individual patient needs.
  • Air-Vent for Uninterrupted Flow:
    Equipped with an efficient air-vent mechanism, the SS-3063 infusion set
    facilitates uninterrupted fluid flow during the entire course of
    intravenous therapy. This feature ensures a continuous and smooth
    infusion process, minimizing the risk of air entrapment and related
    complications.
  • Needle with Thin Wall Technology:
    The infusion needle is crafted with thin-wall technology, optimizing the
    puncture process. This not only enhances patient comfort during
    insertion but also ensures a seamless and efficient delivery of fluids,
    contributing to a more positive patient experience.
  • Roller Clamp for Flow Regulation:
    The inclusion of a roller clamp allows healthcare professionals precise
    control over the flow of fluids. This manual adjustment feature is
    invaluable in situations where minute changes in infusion rates are
    necessary, providing doctors with the flexibility required for a
    tailored approach to patient care.
