What's in the Box

Oxygen Blender

The Korrida Oxygen Blender (KM-KY01) is an advanced medical device designed to precisely control oxygen concentration and flow for neonatal and pediatric patients, particularly premature infants. It helps prevent oxygen toxicity and associated complications such as retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) and chronic lung disease (CLD) by ensuring the accurate mixing of oxygen and air.

This pneumatically controlled device operates without requiring an external power supply, making it highly reliable and maintenance-free. It offers continuous and direct adjustment of oxygen concentration without requiring complex calculations. Additionally, it features an unbalanced alarm for gas supply pressure and supports optional expansions, such as Bubble CPAP and dual-output functionality.

Key Features

  • Independent adjustment of oxygen concentration and flow without interference.
  • Pneumatic and air-controlled operation (no external power supply required).
  • Continuous and direct oxygen concentration adjustment for precise control.
  • Constructed from special alloy materials with anodic oxidation surface treatment for durability.
  • Unbalanced gas supply pressure alarm ensures patient safety.
  • Long lifespan, maintenance-free operation, and no material consumption.
  • Expandable to support Bubble CPAP therapy.
  • Optional flow meter or inhaler attachment for customized use.
  • Dual-output option available for expanded use.
