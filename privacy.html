Privacy & your enquiries

Information for product enquiries, visits and orders.

Information you choose to share

Enquiry forms collect the contact, organisation, product, delivery or support information you enter. This storefront prepares a draft locally; it does not automatically submit the draft to an enquiry server.

Browser storage

Your quote basket and saved product IDs are stored in local storage. The quotation contact draft is stored in session storage for the review step and can be discarded on that screen. Closing the browsing session normally clears session data. Contact, support and meeting form reviews are held in the page until you leave or reload.

Email, WhatsApp & downloads

You choose whether to open an email draft, open WhatsApp or download a text copy. Details are passed to the application you select. You review and send the message there. The selected service and receiving team handle the sent enquiry under their own terms.

External services

Map, social, catalogue and source-company links open external sites. Web fonts may load from Google Fonts. Hosting providers may retain ordinary technical and security logs. Avoid entering patient records, passwords, payment card information or other sensitive material in enquiries.

Information requests

Contact info@drsmith.co.in or +91 9289976683 about correction or removal of enquiries held by the receiving team. Records required for quotation, fulfilment, security or legal obligations may need to be retained.
